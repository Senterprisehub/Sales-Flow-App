**Sales-Flow-App**


                    SALES FLOW - FULL FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION
         Smart Sales Target, Achievement & Executive Analytics Companion
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
Sales Flow is a high-precision, modern Android application built for sales 
professionals, distributors, business owners, and regional managers who want 
complete clarity over their sales targets and actual achievements. Designed 
around Indian numbering conventions (₹, Thousands, Lakhs, Crores) with full 
2-decimal precision, Sales Flow transforms daily sales entries into actionable 
Monthly, Quarterly, and Yearly business intelligence.

Whether you manage multiple client parties, diverse product lines, or complex 
target adjustments, Sales Flow gives you real-time visual dashboards, 
executive-ready PDF reports, AI-driven strategic insights, and effortless CSV 
portability—all stored securely on your device.


================================================================================
KEY HIGHLIGHTS THAT SET SALES FLOW APART
================================================================================

1. EFFORTLESS & FLEXIBLE SALES ENTRY
   - Record Targets & Achievements Separately or Together: Log a monthly target 
     at the start of the month (with 0 achievement) and add achievement logs 
     throughout the month (with 0 target)—Sales Flow automatically aggregates 
     them for accurate attainment percentages.
   - Full Decimal & Negative Adjustment Support: Enter exact fractional figures 
     (up to 2 decimal places) as well as negative values (e.g., -150.25) to 
     account for credit notes, sales returns, or target revisions.
   - Smart Autocomplete & Directory Management: Pick from saved Party Names 
     and Product Categories with instant search suggestions, or add new ones on 
     the fly.
   - Live Attainment Preview: See your real-time achievement percentage badge 
     update dynamically while typing your numbers before you even hit Save.

2. GRANULAR ENTRIES & TRANSACTION LEDGER
   - Multi-Dimensional Filtering: Filter your daily and monthly records 
     instantly by Year, Month, Product Category, Multi-Select Party Chips, or 
     free-text Search across notes, parties, and products.
   - Instant KPI Summary Strip: View filtered Total Target, Total Achieved, 
     Remaining Gap/Surplus, and Overall Attainment % at the top of your ledger.
   - Quick Edit & Delete: Modify any historical transaction or remove outdated 
     entries with a single tap.

3. COMPREHENSIVE YEARLY ANALYSIS DASHBOARD
   - Executive KPI Cards: Track Annual Target, Annual Achievement, Net Variance, 
     Best Performing Month, and Overall Attainment Rate at a glance.
   - Interactive Visual Charts:
     * Monthly Target vs. Achievement Bar & Trend Charts
     * Quarterly Performance Breakdown (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4)
     * Party-Wise Contribution & Share Distribution
     * Product-Wise Performance & Attainment Rankings
   - Deep-Dive Filters: Slice the entire yearly dashboard by a specific Party 
     or Product to evaluate individual client or SKU performance across all 12 
     months.

4. DEDICATED QUARTERLY ANALYSIS DASHBOARD
   - Quarter-Specific Drilldown: Inspect Q1 (Jan-Mar), Q2 (Apr-Jun), 
     Q3 (Jul-Sep), or Q4 (Oct-Dec) performance with dedicated monthly splits.
   - Party & Product Leaderboards: Identify top revenue contributors, share 
     percentages, and target shortfalls within any selected quarter.

5. EXECUTIVE PDF REPORT GENERATOR (ONE-TAP EXPORT & SHARE)
   - Generate clean, multi-page, print-ready PDF reports directly from your 
     phone and share them via WhatsApp, Email, or Drive.
   - Available PDF Report Modes:
     * Filtered Entries & Transaction Ledger Report
     * Complete Yearly Executive Analysis Report
     * Detailed Quarterly Breakdown Report
   - Every PDF includes formatted KPI summary banners, Monthly/Quarterly 
     tables, Party-wise & Product-wise share tables, and complete 2-decimal 
     currency and percentage precision.

6. SMART AI EXECUTIVE INSIGHTS
   - Turn raw numbers into strategy with built-in AI analysis tailored to your 
     sales ledger.
   - Choose specialized focus lenses—such as Overall Executive Summary, Party 
     Performance & Risk, Product Mix Optimization, or Seasonal & Monthly 
     Trends—to uncover growth opportunities and bottlenecks in seconds.

7. CENTRALIZED PARTY & PRODUCT DIRECTORY MANAGEMENT
   - Rename or clean up Party Names and Product Categories from a central 
     manager; Sales Flow automatically cascades and updates all matching 
     historical entries across your database so your reports stay consistent.

8. SEAMLESS CSV BACKUP, SYNC & EXCEL COMPATIBILITY
   - One-Tap CSV Export: Export your entire sales database to a standard CSV 
     file compatible with Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.
   - Smart CSV Import: Restore backups or bulk-upload sales data from external 
     spreadsheets effortlessly.
   - Offline-First Reliability: Powered by a fast local database so you can 
     log sales and generate reports anywhere—even without an internet 
     connection.
